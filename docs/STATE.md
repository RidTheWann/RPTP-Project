## Scope and blockers

The accepted Gate 0 scope is the synthetic ELF32/EM_MIPS fixture and only ADDIU, ADDU, LW, SW, BEQ, BNE, J, JAL, JR and NOP plus tested delay slots. Every later differential (through the ordinary branches), the IR/CFG, known-edge reachability and lowering work are post-disposition Gate 3 work: no runtime-reachability, dead-code-proof, function-extent, product-runtime or compatibility claim.

1. **Provenance reviews (open):** the owner must decide whether the recorded ISO review satisfies the Gate 2 prerequisite; independent reviews of the Gate 0 and build/CI/doctor components remain release prerequisites.
2. **Gate 2 (open):** multi-extent and ISO-variant review plus the owner's disposition; interleaved layouts are rejected even though the BP 27/28 field mapping is spec-checked.
3. **Clean-room/source exposure (policy resolved):** prior exposure is documented in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) and ADR-0001; ADR-0003 seals the affected projects and is not a legal guarantee.
4. **License (resolved):** GPL-3.0-or-later confirmed 2026-10-21; SPDX headers cover project source, build and workflow files.
5. **Renderer (open):** none selected or tested; ADR-0002 remains a plan.
6. **Dependencies:** no runtime dependency is vendored or linked; record license, version and notices before adding one.
7. **Path divergence (open, `R-031`):** the Gate 0 spike, `tools/rptp`, the older `rptp_ir`/`rptp_lower` pipeline and the C++ harness model instructions separately. Branch-likely, the six ordinary forms and the `LW`/`SW` alignment policy agree across `tools/rptp`, the harness and the cross-path suite (PRs #41/#43/#51), but the older `rptp_ir`/`rptp_lower` path rejects the ordinary forms; ADR-0005 (Proposed) awaits the owner decision.
